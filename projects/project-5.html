Engaged as consulting electrical engineer to redesign the power infrastructure for a fishery harbour development, phased at a total planned capacity of 500kVA with Stage 1 proceeding at 250kVA.

Scope of Work

  • Redesigned the main distribution panel, consolidating what had been planned as separate units into a single integrated panel with an incomer Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) and manual changeover for bypass operation
  • Redesigned the broader electrical system layout to suit actual site conditions and constraints
  • Performed load calculations to validate capacity requirements against connected loads
  • Carried out cable sizing for the redesigned distribution
  • Coordinated directly with the client and contractor throughout design and site execution
  • Conducted site inspections to verify installation against the revised design

Power Source

Two 250kVA generators providing the site's primary supply, with the redesigned panel managing automatic and manual changeover between sources.

Skills Demonstrated

Generator-backed distribution design, ATS/changeover system design, load calculation, site-driven redesign, client and contractor coordination.
