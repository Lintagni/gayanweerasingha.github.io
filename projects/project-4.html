Worked as the on-site Electrical Engineer for a private hospital building project in Dehiwala, covering the full site-delivery cycle from coordination through material procurement.

Scope of Work

  • Acted as site engineer for the electrical package, managing day-to-day coordination between subcontractors and the client
  • Conducted regular site inspections to verify installation quality against approved drawings and specifications
  • Arranged and led coordination meetings, preparing formal meeting minutes for record and follow-up
  • Prepared material submittals and method statements for client/consultant approval
  • Collaborated directly with the Revit modeling technician to detail the electrical BIM model and resolve clashes during design development
  • Coordinated design changes as they arose during construction, ensuring drawings and site execution stayed aligned
  • Managed material ordering in coordination with the site store keeper, tracking procurement against the construction program

Skills Demonstrated

Site supervision, BIM coordination (Revit), submittal preparation, contractor management, procurement coordination.
