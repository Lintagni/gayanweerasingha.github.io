This project covered the electrical system design for a new Cardiac Catheterisation Laboratory and its associated clinical rooms at Karapitiya National Hospital. A cath lab is a critical-care medical location, so the design prioritised safe, reliable distribution to sensitive imaging and life-support equipment. Power for the new lab was drawn from the hospital's existing busbar trunking system, so my work focused on the distribution downstream of the busbar tap-off — the boards, circuits, cabling, lighting and BOQ that turned that supply into a working, code-compliant installation.

Scope of Work

·        Tap-off and LV distribution from the existing busbar system

·        Distribution board design — DB-01 and DB-02

·        Load estimation and maximum demand calculation

·        Cable sizing and protective device selection to BS 7671

·        Lighting design in DIALux

·        Earthing and bonding for the medical location

·        BOQ preparation and vendor/consultant coordination

Design Highlights

·        Supply: fed from the existing busbar trunking via a tap-off feeding MDB

·        Distribution: DB-01 serving 2 three-phase AC units, Lights and Power Inside patient room; DB-02 serving 3 AC units, Lights and Power Other areas

·        Lighting: DIALux design at 2.8 m ceiling, surface-mounted 600×600 mm 18 W LED panels, 4000K neutral white; lux target; Patient Room 01 (44.1 m²) with panels

·        Cabling: sized to BS 7671 for current-carrying capacity, voltage drop and disconnection time

My Responsibilities

I was responsible for the electrical design of the Cath lab and associated rooms — preparing electrical layouts, load and maximum-demand calculations, cable sizing, distribution board design, DIALux lighting design, and BOQ preparation, and coordinating with consultants, medical equipment vendors and site engineers.

Tools & Standards
