Delivered electrical engineering design and consulting across a portfolio of 10–20 hospital ward renovation projects for government and private hospitals in the Southern and Western provinces of Sri Lanka, as part of ongoing infrastructure upgrade work.

Each renovation involved reassessing existing electrical infrastructure against current standards and patient-care requirements, then designing updated distribution, lighting, and power layouts suited to the ward's clinical function.
